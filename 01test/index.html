Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using technology and the internet responsibly, safely, and respectfully.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law means the rules and laws that apply to how people use technology and the internet.

What is AUP?

It is a set of rules for how you are allowed to use a school's, company's, or organization's technology and internet.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is when you use someone else's words, ideas, or work and pretend they are your own without giving them credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty means cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork.

What are the rules for AI generated content?

AI can be a tool, but you need to follow your school's rules and be honest about how you used it.
